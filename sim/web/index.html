BIOBUZZ Simulator 19859 setup practice
Driver Station 19859-RC Robot -- 12
No OpMode selected
Gamepad: plug in a PS4/PS5 controller, press any button, then options + cross to make it gamepad 1 (options + circle for gamepad 2), like the real Driver Station.
Keyboard (menu → use keyboard): X cross, O circle, Z square, T triangle, 1 L1, 2 R1, 3 L2, 4 R2, 5 share, 6 options, 7 PS, 8 touchpad, arrows d-pad, WASD left stick, IJKL right stick.
Controller Hold a button with the mouse, drag the sticks. Shift-click a button to latch it down.
